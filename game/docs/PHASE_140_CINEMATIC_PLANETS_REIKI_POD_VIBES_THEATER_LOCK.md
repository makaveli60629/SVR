# Phase 140 — Cinematic Planets + Reiki Pod + Vibes Theater Lock

## Scope

Game-side only. Website, public site, backend, and database files are untouched.

## Lobby scale

- Expanded lobby circumference by changing `ROOM_RADIUS` from 32 to 42.
- Increased wall height from 16 to 18 for a larger presentation space.

## Planet / sky system

- Added a new cinematic Earth/Moon/Mars module.
- Earth is larger and moves much slower around the lobby.
- Moon follows Earth in a slower local orbit.
- Mars follows Earth farther out and varies distance so it appears to come closer and drift farther away.
- Procedural high-resolution textures were added for Earth, Moon, and Mars.
- Old planet systems, old guide lines, large duplicate planet sprites, and high sky lights are hidden.
- Added a star field and soft blue aura atmosphere.

## Reiki storefront

- Keeps Phase 139 uploaded Espresso and Trueitive assets.
- Adds a glass storefront entrance around the Reiki hub.
- Replaces the prior steel-style rope with classic gold stanchion poles.
- Adds sagging red velvet ropes from pole to pole down the red carpet.
- Adds a longer narrow red carpet runner with trim.
- Adds plants behind the ropes on both sides.
- Adds glowing neon chakra wall icons.
- Adds an interactive Reiki hologram pod.
- The hologram display stays off until the user gets close.
- The pod can route the user into the meditation room.

## Vibes Theater

- Adds a blue Vibes Theater storefront opposite the Reiki side.
- Uses theater geometry, blue glass, blue carpet, screen panel, and marquee signage.
