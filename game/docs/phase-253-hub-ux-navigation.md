## Controls

- Keyboard: Arrow keys or WASD move focus.
- Keyboard: Enter or Space selects.
- Controller: Stick or D-pad moves focus.
- Controller: Trigger or A selects.
- VR hands/controllers: point at card and select.
- Shift+H: show navigation help.
