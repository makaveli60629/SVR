## Scope

Game-side only. Website, public site, backend, and database files are untouched.

## Planet visibility correction

The Phase 140 planets were too high/far for lobby visibility. Phase 141 adds a new closer showcase system:

- Earth radius increased to 32.
- Moon radius increased to 9.4.
- Mars radius increased to 12.2.
- Earth orbit radius reduced to 92 so the planets are visible from the lobby.
- Earth orbit speed reduced to `t * .0042`.
- Moon follows Earth at `t * .030`.
- Mars follows Earth at `t * .014` and varies distance so it moves closer and farther away.
- Old planet groups, guide lines, old sky sprites, and high sky lights are hidden.
