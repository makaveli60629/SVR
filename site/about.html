About SVR

Four Hubs. One Social VR Platform.

SVR Poker is being built as a professional social VR destination around play-money poker, storefront-style partner hubs, sponsor visibility, and community-centered experiences.

The public launch page remains the protected front door. This About page explains the business structure behind the platform: four hub stores, founder/partner placement, approval-safe branding, and an AWS-only data direction for future analytics, messages, and admin tools.

AWS data directionPlay-money positioningPartner approval safeModular hub stores

Founder Platform

SVR Poker is the central brand and operator for the lobby, poker experience, sponsor ecosystem, and hub-store network.

Founder/owner records, partner approvals, and live business data are intended to be managed through the private admin system, not exposed on the public launch page.

Hub Store Model

Approved structure

Each hub can have its own storefront, founder/partner story, logo area, website link, store category, and future private VR destination. Unapproved partner details stay marked as pending until approved.

Founder & partner pages

Each hub is designed to support a professional founder/partner profile with biography, services, logo, website link, and approved store collection.

AWS-only data rule

Future visitor counts, messages, admin status, store records, sponsor inquiries, and analytics should route through AWS-backed API/database services only.

Public page protected

The root public Matrix launch page is not changed by this About page. Public visitor counts remain hidden from visitors and visible only inside admin.

Partnership Standard

Professional approval flow

SVR can present partner opportunities without exposing unapproved names, logos, founder photos, URLs, or sponsor claims. Live partner assets should be added only after approval and stored as modular site/game assets.

What a partner hub can include

Logo placement, founder story, approved website link, storefront products, private VR room, sponsor panels, contact button, future campaign tracking, and admin-reviewed inquiries.

What stays private

Visitor counters, admin logs, partner lead review, message history, raw analytics, AWS credentials, payment secrets, and database records remain private to the admin panel.
