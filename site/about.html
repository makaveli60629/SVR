About SVR

Social VR, Rebuilt

SVR Poker is a social entertainment hub built around play-money poker, high-end sponsor placements, immersive storefronts, and community-driven experiences.

The brand combines the energy of poker with the reach of VR: players gather around tables, sponsors receive premium placements, members discover events, and partners can connect their story to the SVR world.

Entertainment

Fast, social, neon-styled VR poker designed for browser and headset access.

Partnership

Storefronts, billboards, sponsor events, and feature pages give brands visible placement.

Purpose

The brand direction includes animal welfare, homelessness support, access, and community campaigns.
