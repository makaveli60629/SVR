About SVR

A social VR poker platform built for play, purpose, and impact.

SVR Poker is being built as more than a poker table. It is a play-money social VR poker experience, a sponsor-supported destination hub, and a modular platform for future community-impact campaigns.

The goal is to bring people together through the love of poker, immersive rooms, sponsor events, private hubs, and storefront portals while keeping the public posture clear, polished, and legally reviewable.

Poker First

Playable Social Tables

Stable poker, readable controls, clean table flow, and a smooth lobby remain the core priority.

Purpose

Community Direction

SVR is structured so sponsorships, events, and future campaigns can support animal shelters, poverty relief, homelessness support, and local community help.

Platform

Modular Hubs

Sponsor spaces, Reiki, PGA, store, lounge, admin, analytics, and private rooms stay modular and removable.

Why “All In”

“All In” is the poker phrase, but it is also the promise: build the game, protect the platform, support the community, and create something bigger than a standard lobby or card table.
