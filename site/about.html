About SVR

Social VR Poker With a Community Mission

SVR Poker is being built as a social VR poker and destination hub. The project focuses on play-money poker, sponsor-supported events, private rooms, storefront portals, and community-impact campaigns.

The long-term direction is to create an immersive entertainment platform that can support charitable giving, animal welfare, homelessness support, poverty relief, wellness spaces, and access to social experiences for people who may be unable to go outside or attend events in person.

Poker First

Playable Tables

The game priority is a stable, readable, playable poker table with clear controls and a polished lobby experience.

Modular Hubs

Partner Rooms

Reiki, PGA, sponsor, store, lounge, and private-room areas are designed as removable modules that can grow safely.

Impact Direction

SVR is structured to support future transparent fundraising and sponsor-backed community programs.
