About SVR

Four Hubs. One Social VR Platform.

SVR Poker is being built as a professional social VR destination around play-money poker, storefront-style partner hubs, sponsor visibility, and community-centered experiences.

The public launch page remains the protected front door. This About page explains the business structure behind the platform: hub stores, founder/partner placement, approval-safe branding, and future data direction for analytics, messages, and admin tools.
