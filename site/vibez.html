What VIBEZ Is

VIBEZ is planned as a short-form video platform for free expression, creative posting, social reactions, and creator discovery. The first version will focus on a working MVP: accounts, video upload, feed browsing, likes, comments, follows, sharing, reports, and admin removal tools.

  • Creator accounts: usernames, profiles, bios, avatars, and creator pages.
  • Video feed: mobile-first vertical browsing with new posts first for the MVP.
  • Engagement: likes, comments, follows, shares, favorites, and view counts.
  • Safety/admin: reports, content removal, user suspension, and audit logs before public beta.

Cloud Separation Rule

The VIBEZ app must not share the SVR Poker game storage or database. It should use its own AWS resources so the sponsor/app module can grow, pause, or be removed without interfering with the poker platform.

  • Separate S3 bucket: future VIBEZ uploads and thumbnails stay isolated.
  • Separate database: app users, videos, comments, reports, and admin actions stay outside the poker game.
  • Separate deploy track: website ad module first, full app repository second.
  • Cost controls: upload limits, budget alerts, and moderation before any public upload launch.
