Impact direction

SVR’s long-term mission is to use play-money poker, sponsorships, events, and advertising to support community-focused causes and access to experiences for people who may not be able to get outside easily.

Giveback categories

  • Animal welfare
  • Homelessness and poverty relief
  • Access to immersive experiences
  • Community support and wellness partnerships

Transparency path

Future dashboards should show sponsor campaigns, event activity, donation totals, partner notes, and impact stories. This phase creates the public placeholder structure.
