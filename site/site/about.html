Mission

SVR Poker is a social VR poker and destination-hub platform built around play-money entertainment, sponsor-supported experiences, community rooms, and future giveback programs.

What SVR is

  • Play-money VR poker and social lounge experience.
  • Storefront portal system for sponsor and partner hubs.
  • Private destination rooms such as poker, golf, lounges, wellness, and store showrooms.
  • Website + VR portal strategy for browsing, support, membership, and future store integration.

Compliance position

SVR is positioned as a play-money entertainment and sponsor ecosystem, not an unregulated gambling product. Any tournament, prize, or monetization model must receive attorney review before public activation.

Platform direction

Poker first

The poker core remains the priority before expanding visuals or additional modules.

Modular hubs

Each sponsor/storefront/private scene must be removable and swappable without damaging the rest of the site or game.

Enterprise path

Admin, analytics, support, membership, store, and backend services are being structured for scale.
