Phase 423 Advertising

One Campaign, Multiple Approved Surfaces

SVR advertising is structured so approved creative can be reused across website placements, store features, event campaigns and future VR surfaces without mixing campaign logic into poker core.

Every live campaign should have a durable campaign ID, approved creative, placement inventory, dates, budget/price terms, attribution events and any required sponsored-content disclosure.

Inventory Preview

Reusable placements

These slots are presentation inventory, not proof that a sponsor contract or paid campaign is active.

Website Display

Approved banner blocks can appear on sponsor, store, event and contact routes with campaign IDs attached to view/click events.

VR / Unity Surface

Approved creative maps to billboard or signage asset IDs. Unity should consume campaign content without owning sponsor billing or payout calculations.

Campaign Story

Events, causes and products can be associated with a campaign only after public claims, brand rights and disclosure requirements are approved.

Campaign Contract

Minimum activation record

Identity

Campaign ID, sponsor/client ID, owner and contract/proposal reference.

Inventory

Approved routes/surfaces, creative IDs, start/end window and impression/click events.

Money

Rate or contractual formula, invoice status, collected revenue and reconciliation stay in business/accounting authority.

Compliance

Claims review, brand/IP permission and clear material-connection disclosure when required.

Measurement funnel

Reach/impression → engagement → click → landing session → lead → qualified lead → proposal → signed client → billed revenue → collected revenue → renewal. Presentation metrics must never be reported as collected revenue.
