Phase 423 Sponsorship

Marketing Inside a Social Poker World

SVR can offer sponsors a modular path from website awareness to approved store placement, event campaigns, hub partnerships and future VR/Unity billboards.

A sponsor does not become active because a logo or JSON record exists. Live placement requires an approved sponsor record, signed terms, brand/IP permission, campaign dates, creative approval, disclosure rules and accounting treatment.

Disclosure standard

When SVR, a creator, partner or spokesperson has a material relationship to a promoted brand, campaign configuration must carry the approved disclosure requirement rather than relying on followers to infer the relationship.
