Partners

Sponsorship, Branding, and Advertising

SVR is designed for modular sponsor placements: storefront hubs, private rooms, event banners, replay jumbotrons, product showcases, and campaign pages.

Storefronts

Lobby Hub Placement

Sponsors can appear as clean storefront portals that lead to private branded spaces without cluttering the main lobby.

Events

Tournaments + Campaigns

Sponsor-backed play-money tournaments and community campaigns can support visibility and giveback objectives.

Media

Jumbotron + Panels

Replay screens, wall panels, and call-to-action boards can be rotated without rebuilding the full game.

All sponsor branding should remain modular and removable until approved. Unapproved branding must stay off by default.
