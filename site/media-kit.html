Audience path

Discover

Visitors see SVR through the public page and polished site pages.

Register

Players and partners join the server-backed early access list.

Explore

Users preview rooms, store items, tournament concepts, and partner hubs.

Engage

Campaigns can route users to sponsor pages, store placements, or contact forms.

Placement controls

Every sponsor surface should remain approval-based and removable. SVR can add, pause, swap, or retire sponsor content without damaging the rest of the website or game structure.

Public pages should only say “server” or “secure system.” They should never reveal the database vendor, cloud provider, keys, or backend location.
