FAQ

Questions Before Launch

Clear answers for players, sponsors, partners, and supporters while SVR Poker is still under active development.

Game

Is SVR Poker gambling?

SVR Poker is positioned as a play-money social VR poker experience. Real-money gambling is not offered from the public website.

Sponsors

Can sponsors fund prizes?

Future sponsor-funded promotions may be explored only with official rules, disclosures, eligibility terms, no-purchase-required access where required, and legal review before activation.

Donations

What does support funding cover?

Support helps keep the site alive during development and supports expansion costs while the formal backend, store, and impact tracking systems are built.

More Answers

Are secret keys public?

No. SQL credentials, API keys, Stripe secrets, and admin passwords must remain server-side only.

Why do pages say preview?

Membership, store, admin, and sponsor systems are visible for layout review first. Live wiring comes after backend security is ready.

Can partners join later?

Yes. Sponsor, Reiki, PGA, store, and private-room concepts are modular so approved partners can be added without breaking the site.
