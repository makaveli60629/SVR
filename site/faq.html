Common questions

Is SVR Poker gambling?

SVR is publicly positioned as play-money entertainment and a social VR platform. Prize or sponsor campaigns need separate rules and legal review before activation.

Can sponsors fund real-money giveaways?

Potentially, where legal, as no-purchase-required sponsor promotions with official rules, eligibility terms, and disclosures. They should not be framed as poker gambling winnings.

Why Cash App?

Cash App $SVRhelp supports development and expansion costs while the formal backend and campaign systems are built.

Where do partner stores live?

All partner stores route through the SVR Store Hub first. SVR remains the central gateway and approval structure.
