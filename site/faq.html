What is SVR Poker?

A social VR poker platform with sponsor placements, events, store concepts, and impact storytelling.

Can sponsors advertise?

Yes. Sponsors can be featured through website banners, event pages, storefront placements, and future VR billboards.

What is the store for?

SVR items, avatar concepts, digital collectibles, sponsor products, and event drops.

What is the mission?

To build an entertaining VR poker hub that can also support community causes and partner campaigns.
