Built for Play, Purpose, and Impact

SVR Poker is designed as a play-money VR poker and social hub where entertainment, sponsorship, tournaments, digital storefronts, and partner rooms can support a larger giveback ecosystem. The long-term goal is to build a platform that helps fund animal shelters, community projects, homelessness support, poverty relief, and future foundation-backed initiatives.
