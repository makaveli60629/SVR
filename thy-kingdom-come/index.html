The vision

One property. Separate missions. Clear accountability.

The campus is being designed so charitable programming, ministry, agriculture and commercial traveler services can operate transparently without mixing funds or responsibilities.

Five-acre pilot

Purchase decision gates

  • 1
    Zoning and permitted uses

    RV park, homes, community facility, agriculture, laundry and small market.

  • 2
    Wastewater feasibility

    Public sewer or an approved engineered alternative.

  • 3
    Survey, access and flood review

    Buildable acreage must be verified before closing.

  • 4
    Utility capacity and cost

    Water, electric and broadband must support phased growth.
